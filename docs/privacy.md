# Heaton Life Privacy Policy

*Effective August 22, 2026. Applies to the Heaton Life app on iOS, iPadOS, macOS, Windows, and Android.*

**Heaton Life does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no tracking of any kind. The app never connects to the internet on its own.

## What stays on your device

Everything you make in Heaton Life — your worlds library, your pattern zoo, snapshots, the evolver's finds, and two viewing preferences (playback speed and whether the tools are shown) — is stored only in the app's own folder on your device and is never sent anywhere.

- **iPhone and iPad:** the folder appears in the Files app under *On My iPhone* (or *On My iPad*) → *Heaton Life*, so you can open, share, or delete those files yourself.
- **Mac:** under the app's Application Support folder (inside the app's container for the App Store version).
- **Windows:** under your AppData folder.
- **Android:** in the app's private storage.

Deleting the app deletes this data. Your device's own backups (iCloud or computer backups on iOS, for example) may include it; that is governed by your device's backup settings, not by the app.

## Things the app does only when you ask

- **Importing** a pattern file or a MergeLife PNG reads the file you chose and nothing else.
- **Paste** (and, where offered, importing from the clipboard) reads the clipboard only when you tap it; on iOS the system asks your permission first. **Copy** writes to the clipboard.
- **Heaton Life Manual** opens a page on GitHub in your web browser. That visit is governed by [GitHub's privacy policy](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement); the app sends no data along with it.

## Permissions

Heaton Life requests no device permissions: no camera, microphone, location, photo library, contacts, or notifications.

## Children

Heaton Life collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Third-party components

Heaton Life is built with the Unity engine, with every Unity service (analytics, advertising, crash reporting, diagnostics) disabled. The file picker on iOS and Android is the operating system's own document picker. No third-party component in the app collects data.
